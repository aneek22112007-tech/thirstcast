Ask ThirstCast (English or हिन्दी)

Answers come only from ThirstCast's data tools. All litres are estimates. Answers can take up to ~25 s.

About and limits

  • Thirstwave: 3 or more consecutive days when reference evapotranspiration (ET0) is above its local 90th percentile for that day of year (1994-2023, ±7-day window).
  • One point per district (district centroid, ~10 km grid). Not field-level.
  • All litres are estimates, shown as ranges (crop Kc ±10%). Lake figures use the full lake or reservoir area, so they are upper bounds.
  • Forecasts are corrected for the offset between the forecast model and the ERA5 archive (days 6-30 ago).
  • Advisory only. Data: Open-Meteo (ERA5, forecast), FAO-56, DataMeet. See Methods.
