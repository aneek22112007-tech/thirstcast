Team LLF · AWS × Bharat · Track 02: Heat and Water

Everyone forecasts how hot the air will be. Nobody forecasts how thirsty it will be. ThirstCast detects thirstwaves and tells you, in litres, what the sky is about to drink.

A 7-day forecast of atmospheric thirst for three Karnataka districts. Every litre is an estimate.

What is a thirstwave?

01

Heat, dry air, wind and sun

Together they set how fast water can leave a crop or a lake.

02

The sky pulls water

That pull is reference evapotranspiration, ET₀, in millimetres per day.

03

Three days above the local p90

A thirstwave is 3 or more days in a row above the local day-of-year 90th percentile.

Why it matters

Heat alerts say the air is hot. ThirstCast says how much faster water is leaving the surface, in litres, for a farmer, a lake manager and a city.

For US cropland, 1981–2021, the research found statistically significant increasing trends over 17% of cropland area for intensity, 7% for frequency and 23% for duration.

Kukal & Hobbins 2025, Earth's Future

This said, according to experts, there is essentially no data about extreme thirstwaves over India.

The Hindu, 24 June 2025
